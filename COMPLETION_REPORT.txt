DATABASE SCHEMA:

  lead_scores:
    - id (UUID)
    - leadId (text)
    - score (0-100)
    - confidence (0-100)
    - decayFactor (0.95 = 5% daily decay)
    - predictedConversion (probability)
    - objectionCounts (JSON)
    - lastUpdated (timestamp)

  sequence_analytics:
    - id (UUID)
    - leadId (text)
    - sequenceKind (post-tour|pre-decision|cold-revival|first-contact)
    - messagesSent (integer)
    - repliesReceived (integer)
    - clickRate (percentage)
    - conversionRate (percentage)
    - status (active|paused|completed|stopped)

  performance_metrics:
    - id (UUID)
    - tcmId (text)
    - date (YYYY-MM-DD)
    - leadsProcessed (integer)
    - toursScheduled (integer)
    - bookingsCreated (integer)
    - conversionRate (percentage)
    - avgResponseTime (minutes)
    - slaCompliance (0-100%)

                          FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS

SMART LEAD ROUTING:
  Scoring factors: zone, conversion, response, load, discipline, intent
  Result: Best TCM auto-selected with explanation
  Location: /zone-brain → Rebalancing recommendations

VISIT WAR ROOM:
  Priority tiers: CLOSE NOW (red) → AT RISK (orange) → FOLLOW-UP → ON TRACK
  Booking probability: 2-98% calculated in real-time
  SLA tracking: 10 checkpoints from T-120 (2h before) to T+60 (1h after)
  Alerts: Critical, warn, info with auto-dismiss
  Actions: Call, WhatsApp, Location share, Movement status

GAMIFICATION:
  XP rewards: 25 for post-tour, 20 for follow-up, 25 for first response, etc.
  Streaks: Daily rollover with local date tracking
  Ranking: Conversion (×1.5 weight) + Discipline - Pending posts
  Revenue: Total team member earnings tracked
  Motivation: Top performers highlighted with trophy icons

LEAD SCORE PREDICTOR:
  Input: 8 factors (intent, response, silence, follow-up, move-in, objections)
  Output: Score (0-100), conversion % probability, decay rate
  Algorithm: Intent multiplier × response/silence/follow-up/move-in/objection scoring
  Decay: 5% per day of silence (cumulative history tracked)
  Fallback: Client-side calculation when API unavailable

SEQUENCE ENGINE:
  Auto-routing: Detects best next sequence based on engagement
  Benchmarks: post-tour (35%), pre-decision (28%), cold-revival (5%), first-contact (42%)
  Metrics: Messages, replies, clicks, conversion rate vs. benchmark
  Rules: No reply after 3 → cold-revival; >70% engagement → pre-decision; etc.
  Stop rules: No reply after 5 touches → escalate to manual
